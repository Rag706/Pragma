Design notes

How the checklist feature works in the drawer above (fully interactive).

1

Progress bar

Auto-calculated from checked items. Updates the bar in the meta card (top) and the checklist section header. Checking/unchecking items animates the bar smoothly.

2

Add & delete items

Click "Add item" or the dashed row. Type and press Enter to save, Esc to cancel. Hover any row to reveal the × delete button on the right.

3

Collapse section

Click the "Checklist" header to collapse/expand the list. The item count badge (3/6) stays visible even when collapsed so you can see progress at a glance.

Checklist progress in the task list row

Tasks with a checklist show a subtle inline progress bar below the title in the task list, so you can see completion at a glance without opening the drawer.
